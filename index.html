About FeedbackIQ

FeedbackIQ stores course feedback using five rating parameters: Teaching Quality, Clarity, Course Content, Assessment and Faculty Interaction. Comments are analysed with keyword-based sentiment classification.

Rating Parameters

Teaching Quality 1–5
Clarity of Explanation 1–5

Faculty Interaction 1–5

How it works

  1. Add student feedback.
  2. Calculate course and faculty averages.
  3. Classify comments as positive, negative or neutral.
  4. Flag courses when any parameter average is below 3.
  5. Generate faculty strengths and improvement areas.
